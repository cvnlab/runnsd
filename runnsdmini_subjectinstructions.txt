Please fixate the central dot throughout the whole run
(even at the beginning and end of the run).

You will see a series of photographs. Your task is to judge
whether or not you have seen each photograph before.

If you have never seen the presented photograph before,
press the first button (INDEX finger = NEW).

If the presented photograph is identical to one that you have seen before,
press the second button (MIDDLE finger = OLD).

The display will occasionally be "blank" for a number of seconds. When this happens,
just keep your eyes on the central dot and wait for the next photograph to appear!

Each run is 6 minutes.
